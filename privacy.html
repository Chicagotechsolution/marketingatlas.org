Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Marketing Atlas is a static informational website. We collect as little data as possible.

What we collect

This site does not set cookies and does not run analytics or advertising scripts. The notification sign-up form does not transmit data anywhere before the first chapter is published; nothing you type leaves your browser.

When sign-ups open

Once the list opens, we will collect only an email address, use it only to announce published chapters, and include an unsubscribe link in every email.

Hosting

The site is served by our hosting provider, which may log standard technical request data (IP address, user agent) to operate and secure the service.
