Review standard

Reviews verified by interview, or not published

The directory's value rests on its reviews, so the standard is set before the first one exists. There are no reviews on Marketing Atlas today — and when they arrive, every one will have passed this process.

Step 1

Client identified

Reviews come from a named client contact at a verifiable company, tied to a specific engagement — never anonymous, never submitted by the agency itself.

Step 2

Analyst interview

An analyst speaks with the client about scope, budget, results, and what they'd change. The write-up comes from that conversation, with the client's sign-off.

Step 3

Published with context

Each verified review shows the project type, budget range, and timeframe alongside the account, so a glowing note on a $3,000 project reads as exactly that.

The pledge: agencies cannot pay to alter, hide, or reorder reviews. Listing is free; verification is mandatory; placement in category tables follows disclosed criteria, not spend.
